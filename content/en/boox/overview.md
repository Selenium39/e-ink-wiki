## What is BOOX?

BOOX is the e-reader product line of Onyx International (Guangzhou Wenshi Information Technology). The biggest difference from Kindle and Kobo: **BOOX devices run open Android**, so you can freely install third-party apps — whereas Kindle and Kobo run locked-down firmware. That locked firmware is exactly why the classic jailbreak guides exist:

- Kindle 5.x software jailbreak & Kindle Touch jailbreak (full guides available in [中文](/zh/wiki/kindle/jailbreak/))
- Kobo Touch / Kobo WiFi hacking (full guides available in [中文](/zh/wiki/kobo/hacking/))

On a BOOX, most of those "jailbreak" steps are unnecessary: installing a third-party reader (such as [KOReader](https://wiki.mobileread.com/wiki/KOReader), whose Android build installs directly), swapping fonts, or running note and office apps are all natively supported.

Market position: after Kindle withdrew from the Chinese market in 2022–2023, domestic brands filled the gap. According to public reporting, Onyx filed for a Hong Kong IPO in January 2026; its prospectus disclosed ~384,000 high-speed reader units sold in 2024, and some global rankings place BOOX among the top e-reader brands by retail value (see [36Kr](https://m.36kr.com/p/3711180856980230) and [Wallstreetcn](https://wallstreetcn.com/articles/3765411), in Chinese).

## Product lineup (as of Nov 2026)

The official store splits the lineup into two main categories, plus an e-paper monitor line:

### High-speed readers

6–7 inch, reading-first devices ([official category](https://zh.boox.com/series/?category=reading)):

| Series | Positioning |
| --- | --- |
| P6 (P6+ / P6 Pro / P6 Pro C) | 7" flagship readers with physical page-turn buttons |
| Leaf (Leaf 5 / Leaf 5C / Leaf 5+) | 7" slim pure readers; the 5C uses a color e-ink panel |
| Poke (Poke7 / Poke7 Pro) | 6" compact entry-level models |

### Productivity tablets

10–13.3 inch stylus-equipped large screens ([official category](https://zh.boox.com/series/?category=ai_working)):

| Series | Positioning |
| --- | --- |
| Note X (Note X5 / X5S / X5 mini / Note X6) | Handwriting notes and document annotation workhorses |
| Tab / T (Tab 10C Pro / T10 C / T10C+ / T13 C) | High-refresh color panels aimed at office work and multitasking |

### Mira monitors

E-paper desktop monitors (e.g. the [MiraPro C color model](https://zh.boox.com/product/?id=4408)) that connect to a computer as an eye-friendly external display.

## BOOXOS

Current BOOX devices ship with **BOOXOS** ("OS4" in official tutorials), customized on top of Android:

- **Open app ecosystem**: install Android apps from the official store, third-party APKs — official tutorials even cover installing Google Play
- **Refresh modes**: multiple e-ink-tuned refresh modes balancing ghosting and speed (see the official "refresh mode" tutorials)
- **Handwriting & notes**: the productivity tablets ship with a stylus and support global handwriting, handwriting-to-text, and speech-to-text
- Kids mode, floating ball, home-screen widgets and other system features all have official tutorials

## NeoReader: the built-in reader

Every BOOX ships with **NeoReader**, the core of the reading experience:

- Broad format support (PDF / EPUB / MOBI / TXT / DJVU / CBZ and more — see the [EPUB](https://wiki.mobileread.com/wiki/EPub) and [MOBI](https://wiki.mobileread.com/wiki/MOBI) format guides on MobileRead Wiki)
- Multiple refresh modes and reading-layout adjustment (font size, typeface, margins)
- Reflow and layout adjustment for PDF / CAJ documents, two-page reading
- Built-in OCR, online dictionaries, highlights, and note export
- 256-level grayscale ("image smoothing") optimization

## Typical use cases

The open Android system enables a few common workflows:

1. **Third-party reading apps**: install WeChat Reading, web-novel apps, and other Chinese reading apps directly
2. **Documents & office work**: large-screen models with a stylus for PDF annotation and meeting notes (official "meeting notes" tutorial)
3. **File transfer**: USB cable, LAN wireless transfer, and the BOOX Assistant app all have official tutorials — far more straightforward than the old Kindle delivery methods

## BOOX vs. Kindle / Kobo

| Need | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| Just reading, minimal fuss, best battery | Kindle / Kobo (see the jailbreak guides on this wiki for tinkering) |
| Chinese reading apps, web novels | BOOX (open system, works out of the box) |
| Handwritten notes, PDF annotation | BOOX Note / Tab series or comparable domestic large screens |
| Tight budget | A used Kindle / Kobo is still the value pick, and the jailbreak guides make it very hackable |
